Readme

How densely are cells packed around each individual cell?

For each cell i, the tool measures the distance ri to its k-th nearest neighbour within the same section. This distance defines a circle in 2D or a sphere in 3D around the cell that contains its k nearest neighbours. A smaller circle or sphere indicates more closely packed cells and therefore a higher local density. Local density is calculated as:

densityi=kπri2

in 2D, and:

densityi=k(4/3)πri3

in 3D.

Map. Each cell is colored by its local density: brighter colors indicate higher density, darker colors lower density, and unpainted cells have no result.

Plot. Cells are sorted by local density. The x-axis shows the percentage of cells, and the y-axis shows the corresponding density. A point at (80%, y) means that 80% of cells have a local density of y or lower, while 20% have a higher density. At 50%, the curve therefore shows the median density.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultWhat it does
Neighbours (k) k Default: 10350Range 3 to 50. Sets how many nearest neighbours are used to estimate local density. Larger values measure density over a broader area and produce a smoother result.
Scale scale Default: log10 density Sets whether density is shown directly or on a log10 scale.
